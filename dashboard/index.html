The question

Bangladesh keeps duty-free access to the EU until about 2032, then loses it unless GSP+ or a deal replaces it. Where should its apparel compete next?

What would change this view

  • An EU trade deal with Bangladesh would keep EU access and make Bet 1 less urgent.
  • US tariffs on Bangladesh above Vietnam's would weaken Bets 2 and 3.
  • The EU-India agreement slipping well past 2027 would ease the pressure in woven garments.
  • Bangladesh's EU share recovering in 2027 would mean 2026's fall was currency and timing, not lost orders.

This is an analysis of public trade data, not investment advice. Assumptions are listed under Method and sources.

Sources

  • EU imports: Eurostat Comext DS-045409, EU27 (2020 composition), annual by HS4 and monthly by chapter.
  • US, UK, Canada, Japan, Australia imports: UN Comtrade public API, reported by the importing country.
  • Tariffs: WITS / UNCTAD TRAINS, MFN applied rates 2022, simple average of HS6 lines in each HS4.
  • Exchange rate: ECB annual average (Eurostat ert_bil_eur_a).
  • Policy dates: UN General Assembly deferral to 24 Nov 2029; EU GSP regulation from 2027; EU-India FTA concluded 27 Jan 2026; US Section 301 tariff of 10% on Bangladesh from 24 Jul 2026. Links in the README.

Data checks (all pass before export)

  • Bangladesh has data in every market and year, 2015 to 2025.
  • Each market's imports from the world cover the five exporters, line by line.
  • EU product detail and 12 months tie to Eurostat's chapter totals (within 0.5%).
  • No negative values; every share is between 0 and 1.
  • Unit values fall in a plausible range, so a unit mix-up cannot reach a chart.
  • Every one of the 34 product lines has an MFN rate in both schedules.
  • EU trade without product detail is sized: 4.5% of 2025, mostly from China.

Assumptions you may disagree with

  • Rules of origin under GSP+: knitwear uses 80 to 90% local fabric and woven 35 to 40%, so 15% of knit value and 62.5% of woven value would fail two-stage origin and pay MFN.
  • Volume effect: a duty becomes a price gap against duty-free Vietnam and India. Lost share = 1 − (1 + duty)−σ, with σ between 2 and 4. This is a rough range, not a forecast.
  • Standard GSP is treated as no help for apparel, because Bangladesh's share of EU GSP apparel imports is far above the graduation threshold.
  • 2025 trade is the base for duty and risk; it ignores growth between now and 2032.
